Ten classic games in one app. Play offline, without ads, purchases or an account. Your games are saved automatically so you can continue later.

SOLITAIRE — Klondike with one-card or three-card draws, hints and an option for deals verified to be winnable.
BLOCK BLAST — Place blocks, clear rows and columns, and build combos.
BREAKOUT — Bounce the ball, break bricks and collect power-ups.
SIRTET — Rotate falling blocks and complete lines as the pace increases.
SUDOKU — Choose a difficulty, make notes, use hints and save checkpoints.
2048 — Slide and merge matching tiles. Keep playing after reaching 2048.
CHARADES — Guess with friends using themed word decks. Use buttons or, on supported devices, tilt controls.
MINES — Uncover safe squares and flag mines. Your first move is always safe.
REVERSI — Flip your opponent's discs. Play against the computer or share a device with a friend.
PIPES — Rotate pipe tiles to connect the whole network.

Menus, instructions and accessibility labels are available in 13 languages. Charades includes localized animal and food decks; the English collection has additional themes.

Open source, built with the Day framework using each platform's native interface. Source: github.com/daybrite/Day-Games
